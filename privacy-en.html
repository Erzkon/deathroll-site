Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the iPhone app “Deathroll – Party Dice” (“the app”) and to this website. The German version is the authoritative one; this English version is provided for convenience.

In short: The app does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no server, no tracking, no advertising and no analytics. Everything you enter in the app stays on your iPhone.

1. Controller

Kevin Bolte
Email: erzkon.dev@gmail.com

2. What data the app processes

The app does not collect any personal data. It does not transfer data to us or to third parties, and it does not sell any data.

  • no user account, no sign-in
  • no server of our own; the app does not connect to any servers operated by the developer
  • no tracking, no analytics, no advertising
  • no third-party software components (SDKs)

3. Local storage on your device

So you don't have to re-enter everything next time, the app stores the following information locally on your iPhone only:

  • the most recently used player names and their order,
  • the most recently chosen start number and the optional stake text,
  • your settings (e.g. sound, haptics, appearance) and whether the introduction has already been shown.

From version 1.2, the app also stores finished games (player names, roll history, mode, start number and an optional stake), the statistics calculated from them, unlocked achievements and a few usage counters (e.g. how often “Who goes first?” was used or a result was shared). All of this data stays exclusively on your iPhone and is never transferred to us or to third parties. You can delete individual games, reset statistics and achievements separately in the app, or remove everything by deleting the app.

The app does not send this information off your device. It may, however, be included in a device backup (e.g. iCloud Backup) if you have set up backups in iOS; Apple's terms apply to those. You can delete all stored information at any time by deleting the app from your iPhone.

4. Sharing a result

At the end of a round you can share the result as an image; from version 1.2 also the turn order drawn with “Who goes first?” or a single achievement. The image contains the player names, the loser, the stake text and some game values. It is only passed on when you explicitly trigger this via the iOS share sheet, and only to the app or recipient you select there (e.g. a messenger). We do not receive any data in the process. The service you choose is responsible for any further processing.

5. Apple as platform provider

The app is distributed through Apple's App Store. When you download and update the app, Apple processes data under its own responsibility, for example your Apple Account. For details, see Apple's privacy policy.

We only receive crash reports and diagnostic data if you have explicitly agreed in the iOS settings to share such data with app developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements). Apple then provides these reports to us in aggregated form, without any link to you personally. You can withdraw this consent there at any time.

6. Contact by email

If you send us an email, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete the message as soon as it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

8. Your rights

Insofar as we process personal data about you (for example in connection with an email request), you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • access to the stored data (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • objection to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR).

To exercise these rights, simply contact the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the authority in your place of residence or the authority responsible for us.

9. Changes

If the app or the legal situation changes, we will update this privacy policy. The version published on this page applies.
