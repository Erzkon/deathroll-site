Deathroll – Party Dice

The quick dice duel for parties and game nights. One iPhone goes around the table – and whoever rolls a 1 loses.

Download on the App Store

What the app can do

  • Classic: take turns rolling as the limit shrinks – whoever rolls a 1 loses.
  • Elimination: roll a 1 and you're out. Play on until only one is left.
  • Statistics: every game counts automatically – ranking, unluckiest player and your head-to-head record.
  • Everyone on their own iPhone: nearby via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, online with a table code via Game Center – and friends without the app (e.g. on Android) join in the browser via QR code.
  • Achievements: 25 achievements for special moments at the table, a few of them secret.
  • Who goes first? Deathroll rolls the turn order for your board games and more.

How it works

The first player rolls from 1 to the start number, say 1,000. The number rolled becomes the new limit for the next player – so it gets tighter with every roll. Whoever rolls a 1 loses.

Kevin rolls 643 (1-1000)
Ben rolls 87 (1-643)
Cara rolls 12 (1-87)
Kevin rolls 1 (1-12)

FAQ

How do I turn off the sound?

In the app, open Settings (gear icon on the start screen) → “Sound”. Deathroll also follows your iPhone's silent switch.

How do I turn off vibration (haptics)?

In the app, open Settings → “Haptics”.

Does the app cost anything?

No. Deathroll is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Is real money involved?

No. Deathroll is a party game. There is no betting with money and there are no prizes. A “stake” is just a line of text you make up yourselves – like who does the dishes.

Can friends with Android play along?

Yes. Whoever opens the table turns on “Allow browser players” in the lobby. The others scan the QR code and play in the browser – no app needed, on the same Wi-Fi or connected to the host iPhone’s Personal Hotspot.

Do I need internet?

Not for “Nearby”: the iPhones connect directly via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, browser players via the local Wi-Fi or the hotspot. For “Online” with a table code, yes – every iPhone needs internet and to be signed in to Game Center.

What data does the app collect?

None. Player names, game history, statistics, achievements and settings stay on your iPhone. See the privacy policy for details.
