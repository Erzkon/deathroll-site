Deathroll – Party Dice

The quick dice duel for parties and game nights. One iPhone goes around the table – and whoever rolls a 1 loses.

How it works

The first player rolls from 1 to the start number, say 1,000. The number rolled becomes the new limit for the next player – so it gets tighter with every roll. Whoever rolls a 1 loses.

Kevin rolls 643 (1-1000)
Ben rolls 87 (1-643)
Cara rolls 12 (1-87)
Kevin rolls 1 (1-12)

FAQ

How do I turn off the sound?

In the app, open Settings (gear icon on the start screen) → “Sound”. Deathroll also follows your iPhone's silent switch.

How do I turn off vibration (haptics)?

In the app, open Settings → “Haptics”.

Does the app cost anything?

No. Deathroll is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Is real money involved?

No. Deathroll is a party game. There is no betting with money and there are no prizes. A “stake” is just a line of text you make up yourselves – like who does the dishes.

What data does the app collect?

None. Player names and settings stay on your iPhone. See the privacy policy for details.
